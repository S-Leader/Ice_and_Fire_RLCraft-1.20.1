Overhaul flying mechanics and AI for Dragons and Hippogryphs

Hippogryph Improvements:
- Fixed a bug where Hippogryphs would suddenly stop flying mid-air and fall to their death.
- Fixed the issue where Hippogryphs would fly upward in extreme spirals into space.
- Smoothed out their flight so they turn and climb much more naturally.

Dragon AI & Flight Overhaul:
- Replaced the clunky, outdated dragon AI with the smoother, classic 1.12.2 RLCraft behavior.
- Fixed the jittering, drifting, and steering issues when a player is riding a flying dragon.
- Fixed the issue where dragons would get stuck swimming underwater; Ice Dragons can now leap out of the water smoothly.
- Improved the escort behavior: Dragons will now predict your movement and fly alongside or ahead of you (great for Elytra flying).
- Fixed the "grounded turret" issue: Dragons will now aggressively launch into the air to breathe fire/ice from above during combat.
- Cleaned up and modernized massive amounts of old code for better overall game performance.
