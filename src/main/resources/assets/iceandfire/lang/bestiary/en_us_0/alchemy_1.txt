Dragon summoning crystals can be
crafted to bind with a dragon,
enabling you to teleport it to
your location.

<item><iceandfire:summoning_crystal_fire 0 15 23 1.25>
<item><iceandfire:summoning_crystal_ice 0 45 23 1.25>



When used on a dragon, the crystal
binds to it. Clicking it again in
the same dimension will teleport
the dragon.

To unbind the crystal, simply place
it alone in the crafting grid.
