Dragon breath can be captured by
holding an empty glass bottle in
either hand when a dragon's breath
or charged blast strikes you. The
captured breath can be thrown or
fired from a dispenser.



Fire dragon breath inflicts Melt,
weakening armor while burning its
victim. Ice dragon breath freezes
its victim. Lightning dragon breath
inflicts Voltage and can leap among
nearby enemies as chain lightning.
