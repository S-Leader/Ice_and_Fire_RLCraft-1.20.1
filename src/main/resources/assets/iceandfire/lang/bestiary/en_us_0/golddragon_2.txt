A mature Gold Dragon possesses a
very unusual breath. It is neither
ordinary dragon flame, frost, nor
lightning, but a stream of magical
energy resembling raw enchantment.

This strange power has one extremely
useful property: it resonates with
every known elemental Dragon Forge.

A Gold Dragon can therefore provide
power to a Fire Dragon Forge, an Ice
Dragon Forge, or a Lightning Dragon
Forge.





For a dragon keeper this makes the
Gold Dragon exceptionally valuable.
A single trained dragon can be used
with all three established forge
types rather than requiring a
different elemental dragon for each
one.

Whatever the true origin of this
golden bloodline may be, its affinity
for knowledge, enchantment, and all
three draconic elements makes it one
of the strangest dragon varieties I
have ever documented.
