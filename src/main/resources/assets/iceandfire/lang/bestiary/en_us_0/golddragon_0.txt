There is a curious rumor among
naturalists. In the corrupted
biomes of the Defiled Lands live
creatures said to be distant
relatives of true dragons.














<image><drawings_golddragon.png 11 62 11 20 180 70 1>

Through careful breeding, these
creatures may very rarely produce
an unusual golden individual.

Old field notes claim that these
golden relatives have a strange
connection to a far greater beast:
the Gold Dragon. Golden specimens
have occasionally been associated
with the appearance of a genuine
Gold Dragon Egg.

The exact reason remains unclear.
Bloodline, accumulated magic, and
the enchanted books these creatures
consume may all play some part in
this extraordinary occurrence.
